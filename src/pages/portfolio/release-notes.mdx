# Release notes

## Sample from major release notes

### See at a glance which record matches are most relevant

:::important
The products are proprietary, so text is slightly modified. Images are mockups of products modified and constructed by Claude.
:::

Reviewing record matches just got faster in the Agency app. Every potential match in the **Needs Review** modal now shows a match relevancy score, so you can instantly tell how likely a flagged record is to be your actual provider before you spend time digging in. Each match displays a simple strength gauge and a clear label:

- **High relevance** means the there is high confidence that the record belongs to the employee, usually because a strong identifier like an ID or Social Security Number lined up.

- **Medium relevance** means it is a probable match worth a look.

- **Low relevance** means it is likely a different employee who shares part of a name, so it is often a false positive.

<SanctionsReviewMockup />

The score appears on every result card across the **Needs Review**, **Accepted**, and **Rejected** tabs, and it works for both employee and applicant matches. Instead of opening every hit to figure out which ones count, you can now prioritize the strong matches first and clear the noise quickly, bringing your review experience in line with what you already get in the Agency Revolution app.
