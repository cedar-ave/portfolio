# Activate or deactivate a template

Activate a template to make it available for your organization's team members to use to generate rosters. As a best practice, don't activate a template until its development is complete. Also, deactivate a template if you stop working with a carrier but want to keep the template for audit purposes. That way, the template can't be used to generate new rosters for that carrier.

**From inside a template**

To activate a template, click the toggle at top right to change it from **Inactive** to **Active**.

To deactivate a template, click the toggle at top right to change it from **Active** to **Inactive**.

**From the list of all templates**

To activate a template, click the dropdown arrow (<FAIcon icon="caret-down" />) at the end of a template's row and select **Activate**.

To deactivate a template, click the dropdown arrow (<FAIcon icon="caret-down" />) at the end of a template's row and select **Deactivate**.

Next, [prepare to generate a roster from the template](./06-generate-a-roster).

## Change a template's name or description

1. Click the pencil icon (<FAIcon icon="pen" />) next to the template name at top left.

2. Replace the text in the **Name** and **Description** fields.

3. Click **Save**.
