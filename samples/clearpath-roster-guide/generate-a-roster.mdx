# Generate a roster

Following are some things to know before generating a roster.

- Data included in the roster only comes from your Clearpath organization. If a carrier requires unique data outside your Clearpath organization, you need to manage it outside the roster.
- To edit a producer's data in a roster, edit the producer's profile in Clearpath and re-generate the roster. You can't edit a roster directly.
- The roster outputs only one row per unique producer that includes all of their associated data.
- You can't preview data in the roster before generating it.

## Steps

Export a roster report to a CSV file using a saved template. The file may need to be manipulated further after export if data outside the Clearpath ecosystem needs to be included.

1. Click the menu icon (<FAIcon icon="grip" />) at top left.

2. Click **View All**.

3. Click **Accounts**.

4. Click the name of an account, which may also be the name of a carrier. If no accounts are visible, select **All Accounts** in the dropdown under **Accounts** at top left.

5. In the **Roster Managed** modal, click **New Report**.

6. Select the roster template to use to generate a roster for the carrier.

 :::tip
A best practice is to give a template the name of the carrier it's for. You can [change a template's name](./manage-templates#change-a-template-s-name-or-description) anytime.
:::

7. Click **Create**.

8. A report takes from 30 seconds to a minute and a half to generate. You receive an email when the roster is generated. Download the file anytime under the **Related** tab in the **Clearpath Roster Reports** modal. In the **Details** tab, see details like the template used, who generated the report, the status of the report, and any errors. To download the exported file, click the file's name in the **Files** modal.

A report is generated by a batch job, which executes the query/export in batches of 200 before combining those queries into a single CSV. This enables the query to avoid heap-size limits when large amounts or producers and fields are in play. The report generation batch job initiates when a job completes.

## Preview the roster

The **Preview** panel at right shows a model of what the roster output will look like. Changes you make to the template are reflected in the preview in real time.

To change a column's width, drag the column's left or right edge in the heading row.

## See and download previous exports

1. Click the menu icon (<FAIcon icon="grip" />) at top left.

2. Click **View All**.

3. Click **Accounts**.

4. Click the name of an account, which may also be the name of a carrier. If no accounts are visible, select **All Accounts** in the dropdown under **Accounts** at top left.

5. See previous exports under the **Related** tab in the **Clearpath Roster Reports** modal. Click a report name to download the exported file, see details like the template used and who generated the report, the status of the report, and any errors.

:::note

An exported roster may require manual manipulation before sharing it with a carrier. For example, a carrier may require a cover sheet on a separate Excel tab, splitting data among multiple sheets, changing a column's data format using Excel, or including data not sourced from Clearpath. Clearpath doesn't currently support these scenarios.

:::
