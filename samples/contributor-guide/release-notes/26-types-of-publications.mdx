## Release notes

Release notes are generated from Azure DevOps work items. See [Work item logic](./27-work-item-logic).

| Subtype | What it is | Work item type | Location of source content | How it's published | How it's refreshed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What's new items | New functionality | Product Backlog Item or Feature | release-notes repo | Local scripts | Local scripts |
| Fixed bug | Fixed bug | Bug | release-notes repo | Local scripts | Local scripts |

:::tip
[How to change, add, or remove release notes](./32-review-release-notes#add-remove-or-change-a-release-note)
:::

## Known issues

Known issues are generated from Azure DevOps work items. See [Work item logic](./27-work-item-logic).

| Subtype | What it is | Work item type | Location of source content | How it's published | How it's refreshed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N/A | Active bug | Bug | Azure DevOps queries (one per Classic edition release) | Build pipelines (one per Classic edition release) | Automatic trigger for the pipelines every four hours |

Product categories with no notes are removed on release notes pages on Clearpath Community. They remain on known issues pages.

:::tip
[How to change, add, or remove known issues](./36-update-known-issues-report)
:::

## Critical and high bugs

Critical and high bugs reports are generated from Azure DevOps work items. See [Work item logic](./27-work-item-logic). The report is separated into two lists: open and closed.

| Subtype | What it is | Work item type | Location of source content | How it's published | How it's refreshed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open in Classic edition | Open bug with a **Critical** or **High** status | Bug | Azure DevOps query | Build pipelines | Automatic trigger for the pipelines every four hours |
| Closed in Classic edition, past 60 days | Closed bug with a **Critical** or **High** status closed in the past 60 days | Bug | Azure DevOps query | Build pipelines | Automatic trigger for the pipelines every four hours |

:::tip
[How to change, add, or remove critical and high bugs](./38-update-critical-and-high-bugs-report)
:::
