# About rosters

A roster is a comprehensive list of all producers who have been appointed by an MGA. The MGA sends a carrier a roster, which the carrier uses to verify that the qualifications of producers meet appointment standards so the producer can start writing business.

MGAs periodically update the roster to reflect changes in producer status, such as new hires, terminations, or changes in licensing. These updates ensure that carriers have current information about the producers appointed under them.

## Producer information included on a roster

Roster requirements may vary by carrier. You can include any of the following producer information:

- General identity and contact information
- Licensing
- Education
- Lines of authority and designations
- National Producer Number (NPN)
- Carrier writing numbers
- Errors and omissions (E&O) coverage
- Territory and book of business info
- Accepting new business status
- Appointment date
