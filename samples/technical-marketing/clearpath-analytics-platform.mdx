# Clearpath Analytics Platform

<Subtitle>The power of the platform.</Subtitle>

The Clearpath Analytics Platform is the breakthrough technology at the foundation of Clearpath's Adaptive-Binding Data Warehouse.

It's not a lack of data that makes it challenging for healthcare organizations to uncover opportunities to lower costs and improve care — just one patient encounter can generate hundreds of rows of data in source systems spanning nearly every area of the organization.

Without a way to organize that data into a data warehouse, volumes of clinical, financial, patient satisfaction, and operational data stay trapped in silos. Leaders end up making critical business and clinical decisions based on only fragments of the big picture.

## Turning data into outcomes

Powered by a unique metadata-driven ETL engine, the Clearpath Analytics Platform extracts data from a healthcare organization's many source systems and gathers it into Clearpath's Adaptive-Binding Data Warehouse, where it binds the right data at the right time, in the right place.

When surfaced later in interactive visualizations, decision-makers can bring that quality, cross-organizational data into focus and deliver specific, actionable interventions in quality, outcomes, patient safety, and waste reduction.

## Benefits

### Rapid time to value

Insights in weeks and months, not years.

### Cross-organizational discovery

- Integrates disparate data across the organization into a single source of truth
- Links data like patient information between source systems
- Shows opportunities for actionable interventions
- Quickly adapts to changing needs and market conditions

### Powerful, intuitive software and tools

Users can:

- Extract, transform, and load data from commonly available clinical, financial, and ancillary sources
- Create, maintain, and customize data marts
- Manage ETL processes
- Provide self-service tools to the front lines
