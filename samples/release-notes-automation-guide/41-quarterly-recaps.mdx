# Quarterly recaps

Each quarter, the platform team publishes a **What changed** blog post in Clearpath Community. These meet a high demand for a high-level overview of a release that is easy for internal team members to digest and appropriate for customer-facing team members to share with customers.

## Steps

- Ask each PM to submit a "press release" of what changed during the quarter.
- Compile these into the **What changed** blog post.
- Supplement with information from Azure DevOps feature work items completed during the release. A helpful framework is slide decks used to capture release objectives. Find these in the **Program Management** folder in the platform team's shared library.
- Include links to supporting demo videos and completed feature requests (both in Clearpath Community) when applicable.

:::note
Examples of raw "press releases" from PMs
:::

In Clearpath Community, tag the blog post with **what changed**. This makes it show up in **What changed** tiles on the landing page of the Classic edition space in Clearpath Community.

### Personas

- Clients
- TDs
- Sales
- ASO
- Engagement leads
- Internal product teams
- Executives

## Characteristics

- Consumable
  - Put "exciting" information relevant to many people at the top
  - Use active voice
- Less emphasis on technical minutiae and more on high-level, easy-to-grasp descriptions of key functionality
- More images and video clips that illustrate "What do you mean?"

## Elements

- **Features**: More descriptive; more screenshots
- **Bugs**: Terse, one-sentence description and title
- **Usability enhancements**: PBIs not tied to features (descriptive info is enough)
- **Progress reports**: How many installs have happened, etc.

## Versions

- Internal (e.g., Azure VMs environments)
- External
