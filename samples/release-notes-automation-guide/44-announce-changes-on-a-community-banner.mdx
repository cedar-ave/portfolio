# Announce changes on a Clearpath Community banner

## Steps

On the Classic edition home page in Clearpath Community:

- Click the pencil at top left > **Manage Group Theme** > **Edit this Page**.
- Hover over the **What's New** widget.
- Click the cog.
- Change the HTML content.
- Click **Save**.
- Click **Publish**.

## Examples

```html
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Classic edition home page, Analytics Catalog, and smarter saving in Template Designer. See a recap of <a href="<community-site-url>/what-changed">what changed</a>.</span>
```

```html
Classic edition 20.2 is released!&nbsp;&nbsp;<a style="font-weight: 400;" href="<community-site-url>/what-changed">What changed</a>&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="font-weight: 400;">|</span>&nbsp;&nbsp;<a style="font-weight: 400;" href="<community-site-url>/download">Download software</a>
```
