# Update critical and high bugs report

Critical and high bugs are bug work items with marked **Critical** or **High** in a work item's **Severity** field, separated into two lists: open and closed.

Whether a bug work item is published as in the list of open critical or high bugs is not determined by the work item's **Publish** field. The list of closed bugs is.

The report is published on the **Critical bugs** pages of the Clearpath Community Releases wiki.

## Lists

- **Open in Classic edition**: Open bugs with a **Critical** or **High** status
- **Closed in Classic edition, past 60 days**: Closed bugs with a **Critical** or **High** status closed in the past 60 days

## Choose work items for the report

Follow the steps in [Fill out work items](./29-fill-out-work-items).

## Add, remove, or change critical or high bug

You can't add or remove a bug from the **Open in Classic edition** list, but you can change the following in both the **Open in Classic edition** and **Closed in Classic edition, past 60 days** lists:

- To change the text, edit the **Release note** field.
- To change which product categor(ies) it's published under, change the **Product(s) Affected** field.
- To change which Classic edition version known issues Clearpath Community page(s) it's published on, change the **Version(s) Affected** field.

To add or remove a bug from **Closed in Classic edition, past 60 days** change the **Publish** field.

Changes are published on Clearpath Community with the next automatic refresh. See [Refresh known issues and critical and high bugs](./40-refresh-known-issues-and-critical-and-high-bugs).
