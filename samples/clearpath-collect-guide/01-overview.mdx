# Overview

Clearpath Collect is a web-based tool for building custom data-entry apps that write directly into your Clearpath data warehouse.

Sometimes a source system doesn't capture a slice of data you need. With Collect, you build and deploy a custom web form to collect it. For example, Collect apps can:

- Fill gaps in a claims or underwriting system's data-collection process
- Serve as a stopgap while a permanent system change is implemented
- Capture custom lists and hierarchies needed for reporting
- Collect data for research and process-improvement initiatives

Data collected flows directly into the warehouse. You can create as many apps as you need, manage them in a central place, and scale them to meet your organization's needs.

## Benefits

- Create and deploy an app in minutes
- Start a research or improvement project without requesting a custom build from IT
- Avoid purchasing and maintaining a separate point solution

With the right practices in place, Collect can end department-level, spreadsheet-based data collection for good.

## Clearpath Collect and the Clearpath platform

Collect is a component of the Clearpath platform, the technology underneath your Clearpath data warehouse. Clearpath extracts data from source systems, gathers it into the warehouse, and maps it for downstream use in reporting and analytics. Collect apps write into that same warehouse.
